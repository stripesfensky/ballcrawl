The Ball Crawl

What is The Ball Crawl?

The Ball Crawl is a community catalog for items related to Pizza Time Theatre, ShowBiz Pizza Place, and Chuck E. Cheese's, providing direct and to-the-point information regarding each brand, its characters, store locations and concepts, stage shows, animatronics, walkaround costumes, showtapes, showtape formats, and showtape transcriptions.

The catalog is designed so that every entry connects to each other. Users will be able to quickly find the information they are looking for by using content trees, category-based navigation, or through basic title and property search. The editor for each entry will differ based on the type of item it is, using existing entries to populate dropdown menus or other form elements.

The underlying system will be designed to encourage community contributions that expand the database, with guardrails to keep the information clean, so that the catalog can coexist with other community resources as a primary source for quick research and reference.
