---
title: Privacy Policy
---
The Ball Crawl does not collect personal data from general site visitors. The site theme you selected is stored locally in your browser's local storage and is never sent to a server.

This site is hosted via **GitHub Pages**, with webfonts provided by **Google Fonts** and **Font Awesome**. Visiting this site sends standard HTTP requests (like your IP address) to these providers, which are handled according to their respective privacy policies.

If you sign in with Discord to submit content, we receive your basic Discord profile (ID, username) to authenticate access, track entry status, and link drafts to your account.

Approved entries are published publicly on the site and credited using your Discord username. Submitted content, review statuses, and moderator notes are stored to manage the publishing process. 

We do not sell or share your data.
