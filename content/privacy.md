## Short and sweet
The Ball Crawl does not collect personal data from you. 

Your selected site theme is stored locally in your browser's local storage and is never sent to a server.

## Third party services
This site is hosted via **GitHub Pages**, with webfonts provided by **Google Fonts** and **Font Awesome**. Visiting this site sends standard HTTP requests (like your IP address) to these providers, which are handled according to their respective privacy policies.
