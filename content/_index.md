## What is The Ball Crawl?

The Ball Crawl is a community catalog for items related to Pizza Time Theatre, 
ShowBiz Pizza Place, and Chuck E. Cheese's, providing direct information about each brand, 
its characters, store locations, stage shows, and animatronics. More categories
will be added over time as the database grows.

The underlying system is designed with guardrails to keep the information clean, so that the catalog 
can coexist with other community resources as a primary source for quick research and reference.
